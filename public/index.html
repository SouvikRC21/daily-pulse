    Automatic sync every evening

    An iPhone Shortcut reads today's Apple Watch numbers from Health and sends them straight to this server at 9 pm. Nothing to paste. The full setup is in the README, and the two values it needs are here.

    Sync address
    Header
    Authorization: Bearer <your token>
    1. Build the Daily Pulse Sync shortcut from the README: Find Health Samples for each number, then Get Contents of URL (POST, JSON) to the address above.
    2. Add Get Dictionary Value report and Show Notification so your iPhone and Apple Watch show the daily report.
    3. In Automation, add Time of Day 9:00 PM, Daily, choose Run Immediately, and pick the shortcut.
